About Mohd Adil Kazmi

Mohd Adil Kazmi is an SEO consultant and the founder of LLM's Marketing, with more than six years of hands-on experience helping websites earn visibility in organic search.

The core of that work is SEO experimentation: making deliberate changes to pages and measuring the effect, rather than relying on rules of thumb or untested advice. Titles, content structure, internal links, technical setup and structured data are all treated as variables to test, not assumptions to repeat.

As AI assistants started answering search queries directly, the same testing mindset carried over to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): looking at how brands are mentioned and cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI search features, and which signals appear to influence that visibility.

On LLM's Marketing, Mohd Adil Kazmi writes practical guides on GEO, AI search visibility, and schema and entity optimization for marketers and business owners who want to stay discoverable as search changes.

How These Articles Are Written

  • Practical first: each guide turns concepts into steps you can apply to your own website.
  • Honest about uncertainty: no article promises guaranteed rankings or AI recommendations. What search and AI platforms document is kept separate from what is observed in testing.
  • Measurable: recommendations point to evidence you can check yourself in Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4 and repeatable prompt tests.
  • Kept current: articles are reviewed and updated as search engines and AI platforms change.
