Our Approach

Built on testing, not guesswork.

Search is changing fast. We keep up the only reliable way: by testing changes on real websites and measuring what actually moves rankings, AI mentions and leads.

01 — Principles

What we stand by.

LLM's Marketing was founded by Mohd Adil Kazmi after more than eight years of hands-on SEO experiments. These four principles come straight from that work.

01

Test before we recommend

Advice is based on what we have measured on real websites, not on rules of thumb or industry hype.

02

Visibility has to convert

Rankings and AI mentions only matter if they lead to enquiries, so every plan connects visibility to lead follow-up.

03

Honest about guarantees

Nobody controls what Google or ChatGPT shows. We are clear about what we can influence and how we will report it.

04

Plain-language reporting

Monthly reports explain what changed, why it matters and what we will do next, without jargon.

02 — AI and People

AI for speed. People for judgement.

We use AI where it saves time and makes the work more thorough. Decisions that affect your brand stay with people.

Where AI helps

  • Researching the questions and prompts your buyers use
  • Checking how AI platforms describe your brand across many prompts
  • Spotting technical and content issues across large websites
  • Answering and routing new enquiries around the clock

What people own

  • Strategy, priorities and what to work on first
  • Final content, fact-checking and brand voice
  • Interpreting results and deciding what to change
  • Every conversation with you
