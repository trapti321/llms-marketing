04 — Local SEO

Local SEO for electricians, done by the rules.

Most electricians travel to the customer, which makes them service-area businesses in Google's eyes. Google's guidelines say service-area businesses should hide their address and list the areas they serve, and that business names should reflect the real-world name, without added keywords. A shop or showroom that is staffed during business hours can show its address and a service area too.

We set your profile up this way, then grow relevance and prominence with complete services, real job photos, a steady flow of genuine reviews and pages for the towns you actually cover. We never use virtual offices or keyword-stuffed names. They break Google's guidelines and can get a profile suspended, which takes you out of the map pack entirely.

Reviews customers trust

We set up a simple routine to request reviews after each completed job and reply to every one. We never buy or write reviews: the FTC's rule on consumer reviews, effective October 21, 2024, prohibits fake reviews and paying for positive ones.

10 — FAQ

SEO for electricians: common questions.

What is SEO for electricians?

SEO for electricians is the work of getting an electrical contractor found when homeowners and businesses search for electrical services nearby, on Google Maps, in organic results and in AI assistants. It covers the Google Business Profile, a page for each service you offer, service-area pages for towns you genuinely cover, reviews, technical fixes and tracking calls back to the searches that produced them.

How long does electrician SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile and technical fixes can show results within weeks. New service pages, reviews and local authority usually take several months to move rankings, and competitive metros take longer than smaller towns. We report monthly on calls and booked jobs so you can see progress before rankings peak. Nobody can guarantee a specific ranking, and we will not.

Should an electrician show their address on Google?

Most electricians travel to customers, which makes them service-area businesses under Google's guidelines. Google says service-area businesses should hide their address and list the areas they serve instead. If customers visit a staffed shop or showroom during business hours, you can show that address and add a service area as well.

Do you offer SEO for electrical contractors who do commercial work?

Yes. Commercial and industrial electrical contractors win work differently: buyers are facility managers, general contractors and property managers who search by project type and compare capabilities. We build project and capability pages, highlight licensing, safety programs and completed project types, and track inquiries and bid requests rather than emergency calls.

Is SEO better than Local Services Ads or Google Ads for electricians?

They do different jobs. Local Services Ads and Google Ads put you in front of customers immediately and charge per lead or per click. SEO builds visibility you do not pay for each time someone calls. Most electricians do best with SEO and the Google Business Profile as the foundation and paid ads to fill gaps, such as a new service area or a slow season.

How much does SEO for electricians cost?

It depends on your market, the services and towns you want to rank for and how much your website needs. A single-truck shop in a small town needs far less than a multi-location contractor in a large metro. We scope every engagement after a free audit, so you see the plan and the price before you commit.
