FitBudd: 3.7x organic users and a 414% jump in ChatGPT referral sessions

FitBudd is a SaaS platform that helps personal trainers, gyms and fitness businesses run their own branded app. The goal was to grow non-paid discovery on Google and to make sure FitBudd shows up when buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini which coaching software to use.

3.7xMonthly organic users, 13K in Apr 2024 to 48.1K in Mar 2025
+86%Google impressions, 10.9M to 20.3M over matched 7 month windows
+25%Google clicks, 190K to 238K over the same windows
+414%ChatGPT referral sessions (2,560) vs the previous period

The problem

FitBudd competes with much larger coaching platforms for buyer searches like “best app for personal trainers”, and AI assistants increasingly answer those questions directly. The job: rank for those searches on Google and be one of the brands the AI answers actually name.

The results

Across April 2024 to March 2025, organic search brought 354.7K active users (up 379.7%) and 459K sessions (up 302.2%). From February to September 2025, AI platforms became a measurable channel: ChatGPT sent 3,700+ sessions across referral and untagged traffic, Perplexity 940+, and Claude, Gemini and Copilot all started sending visits.

ChatGPT visitors who convert: +80% key events in 30 days

We tracked visitors arriving from ChatGPT all the way into the logged-in FitBudd dashboard. They were not just reading. Most of them took a tracked action in the product.

+42%Active users from ChatGPT, 423 to 601
+34%Sessions from ChatGPT, 742 to 994
+80%Key events, 2,418 to 4,346
84.7%Session key event rate, with 88.9% engagement
