AI advertising now means two different jobs. The first is using AI to run your campaigns: targeting, bidding, creative and measurement. The second is newer and changes more: buying ad placements inside the AI answers people read in ChatGPT and Google Search.

Most guides cover only the first job. This one covers both, and it explains the part paid teams often miss. An ad can put you next to an AI answer, but it cannot put you inside the answer itself.

What is AI advertising?

AI advertising is the use of artificial intelligence to plan, buy, create, deliver or measure ads, together with advertising on AI-powered surfaces such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode.

The two sides work differently:

How does AI help you run ad campaigns?

Inside today's ad platforms, AI handles four tasks that people used to manage by hand.

Targeting

Models predict which users are likely to convert from signals such as search terms, page context, past behaviour and your first-party customer data. Your job shifts from picking audiences to feeding the model accurate conversion data.

Bidding and budget allocation

Automated bidding sets a price for each auction in real time against a goal such as target cost per acquisition or target return on ad spend. It works well when the conversion signal is clean and poorly when it counts the wrong actions as wins.

Creative production and testing

Generative AI drafts headlines, images, video cuts and size variations in minutes, and dynamic creative tests combinations automatically. The IAB's 2026 video ad spend report notes that generative AI use in video creative keeps accelerating, while many advertisers still want more proof of performance.

Measurement

AI models help with attribution, forecasting and anomaly alerts. They still depend on what you track, so a broken conversion tag produces confident but wrong optimisation.

The practical rule across all four: AI amplifies the inputs you give it. Clean data and clear goals compound; messy data compounds too.

Where can you advertise inside AI answers today?

Two surfaces matter most for most marketers right now: ChatGPT and Google's AI results.

ChatGPT ads

OpenAI announced its ad test on January 16, 2026, starting in the US with clearly labeled ads placed at the bottom of answers. According to OpenAI's help center, the current rules are:

  • Ads show only to Free and Go users. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts see no ads.
  • Ads do not appear for users identified as under 18, in Temporary Chats, or near health, mental health or political conversations.
  • Advertisers get aggregated performance data such as views and clicks, not users' chats or personal details.

In October 2026, OpenAI added a visual ad format shown during image generation, alongside measurement integrations with conversion, attribution and incrementality partners. Businesses sign up through ads.openai.com.

Google AI Overviews and AI Mode ads

Google does not run a separate product for this. Your existing campaigns become eligible. Google's Ads Help documentation states that:

  • Ads can show above or below AI Overviews in every market where AI Overviews are available.
  • Ads within AI Overviews come from Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, currently in English in markets including the US, India, Canada and Australia.
  • You cannot opt out of or directly target AI Overview placements, and segmented reporting for those ads is not yet available.
  • Sensitive verticals, including finance, healthcare, gambling and politics, are excluded.

For AI Mode, Google says advertisers already running Performance Max, Shopping or Search campaigns with broad match, including AI Max for Search, are eligible to have their ads appear there. eMarketer reports that those ads can show within or beneath AI answers.

Do ads change what AI assistants recommend?

No. OpenAI states that ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers and that the ad system runs separately from the model. Ads sit beside or below the response, clearly labeled.

This matters for budget planning. When someone asks ChatGPT for "the best CRM for a 10-person agency", the brands named in the answer are chosen by the model, not by an auction. An ad can sit under that answer. It cannot add your name to the list.

Being named in the answer itself is an organic outcome, earned through the signals AI systems rely on: clear entity information, consistent brand mentions across trusted sources, and content that answers specific questions well. Earned visibility does not need ad spend to start working. In our FitBudd case study, ChatGPT referral sessions grew 414% between February and September 2025, months before ChatGPT carried any ads.

How should you split budget between AI ads and AI visibility?

Treat AI answers as having two shelves: the answer (earned) and the space around it (paid). The best position is both, because a buyer who sees your name in the answer and your ad beneath it gets two signals in one screen.

Paid reach stops the moment spend stops; earned visibility in AI answers keeps working.

How do you start with AI advertising?

  1. 1. Fix your conversion data. Confirm that tags, offline conversions and value tracking are accurate, because every AI bidding system optimises toward them.
  2. 2. Check where your ads already appear. If you run Performance Max or broad match, you may already be serving next to AI answers. Review search terms and placement data before adding spend.
  3. 3. Audit your organic AI presence. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask and record who gets named. Our AI search visibility guide walks through this.
  4. 4. Run one controlled paid test. Pick a single surface, one offer and a fixed budget, and compare cost per acquisition with your search benchmark over at least four weeks.
  5. 5. Use AI for creative volume, humans for judgement. Let generative tools produce variations; let people decide what is on brand and accurate.
  6. 6. Review monthly. These products change fast, so recheck eligibility, formats and reporting each month.

What are the risks and limits of AI advertising?

  • Thin reporting. Google does not yet separate AI Overview ad performance in reports, so you cannot isolate results for that placement.
  • Less placement control. Placement inside AI answers is mostly automatic, with no direct targeting or opt-out on Google.
  • Category restrictions. Health, finance, politics and other sensitive categories are excluded or limited on both ChatGPT and Google AI answers.
  • Vendor-reported results. Early case studies, such as the lower cost per acquisition OpenAI reports for some partners, come from platforms or advertisers, not independent tests. Treat them as hypotheses to test.
  • Creative quality and trust. Generative creative can drift off brand or state things that are not true. Every AI-made ad needs a human check before it runs.
  • Over-reliance on paid. Ads rent attention next to AI answers. They do not build the brand signals that get you mentioned inside them.

Where should you go from here?

AI advertising is no longer one topic. Use AI inside your ad platforms to work faster and bid smarter, test ads inside AI answers where they fit your audience, and build the organic visibility that decides whether AI assistants name you at all.
