Our research standards

How every report is built.

  • Primary sources firstEvery figure links to the original study, survey or company disclosure. Statistics we cannot trace are left out.
  • Graded evidenceSources are graded A to C for strength, so you can see which findings are solid and which are early signals.
  • U.S. market focusWe prioritize U.S. data and explain what each finding means for American businesses and budgets.
  • Updated when evidence changesReports carry a last-updated date and are revised when new data replaces old figures.
