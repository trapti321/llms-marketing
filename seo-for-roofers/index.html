01 · The Problem

Why good roofers still lose the search.

Roofing demand does not arrive in a smooth line. A hailstorm or a windy weekend can create a month of work in one zip code overnight, while the rest of the year runs on slow, considered replacement decisions. Homeowners in both situations do the same thing first: they search, look at the map pack, read a few reviews, check photos of past jobs and call one or two companies.

The roofers who win those calls are not always the best roofers. They are the ones with a complete profile, a clear page for the job the homeowner needs and proof that real people in the area hired them. SEO for roofers is the work of putting your company in that position before the phone needs to ring, and keeping it there through the quiet months. The work is led by our founder, Mohd Adil Kazmi, an SEO consultant with more than eight years of hands-on search experience.

05 · Storm Season

Ready before the storm, trusted after it.

After a big hail or wind event, homeowners search for storm damage repair, roof inspections and help with insurance claims, and they are wary. The FTC warns that home repair scammers may find homeowners after a flood, windstorm or other natural disaster, pressure them to act quickly and ask for cash. Its advice to consumers is to confirm a company is licensed and insured, get three written estimates and sign a written contract before work starts.

Good storm content works with that advice instead of against it. We build your storm damage and insurance claim pages so they show your license, your insurance, your local address history and photos of past storm repairs, explain how inspections and documentation work, and avoid anything that sounds like "free roof" or deductible promises. That is what separates a local contractor from the truck with out-of-state plates.

A warning about storm-chaser lead pages

Some lead-generation sites spin up a page for every town a storm touched, collect the homeowner's details and sell them to whichever roofer pays. Do not copy that model on your own site. Thin storm pages for towns you have never worked in look exactly like the doorway pattern Google's spam policies describe, they rarely convert, and they put your whole domain's reputation at risk to chase one season.

06 · Local SEO for Roofers

Service-area pages and profiles done by Google's rules.

Google's spam policies define doorway abuse as pages created to rank for similar searches that lead users to less useful pages, and give a specific example: pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page. Fifty "Roof Repair in [Town]" pages with the town name swapped are exactly that. We build service-area pages only where you have real work to show: local projects, the roof types and HOA rules common in that neighborhood, permit notes and reviews from customers there. Towns without that material are covered by your main service pages and your Business Profile service area.

Google Business Profile for a service-area roofer

Most roofers travel to the customer, so they are service-area businesses. Google says that if you don't serve customers at your business address, you should remove it and list your service area instead. You can set up to 20 service areas, and the overall area should not extend beyond about two hours of driving from where the business is based. Google's guidelines also say service-area businesses can't list a virtual office unless it is staffed during business hours, and that adding unnecessary information to a business name isn't permitted. We follow all of this, because a suspended profile in storm season is a disaster no ranking can fix.

07 · Reviews and Photos

Reviews with project photos close the sale.

A new roof is one of the largest checks a homeowner writes, so proof matters more than in most trades. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking, that replying to reviews shows you value feedback, and that photos and videos help tell the story of your business. For roofers, that means a simple routine at the end of every job: before and after photos uploaded to the profile and the matching service page, a review request sent the same day and a reply to every review, good or bad.

We never buy, write or gate reviews. The FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials, effective October 21, 2024, prohibits fake reviews and incentives conditioned on positive sentiment. Our guide on how to get more Google reviews walks through a compliant request process step by step.

08 · Ads and AI Answers

Where SEO fits with Local Services Ads and AI search.

Roofers are one of the categories where Google offers Local Services Ads. Google says advertisers pay only for leads related to their business, and that businesses which pass its screening process earn the Google Verified badge; to start running before the badge, a roofer needs a license, or a business registration where a license isn't required. LSAs are useful for filling gaps after a storm or entering a new area, and they draw on the same reviews and profile strength that SEO builds. Our Google Ads management team runs LSAs and search campaigns judged on cost per signed job, so paid and organic do not fight over the same homeowner.

Homeowners also ask AI assistants which roofer to call or whether a damaged roof needs replacing. Google says there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond sound SEO, so our AI search visibility work builds on the same pages, profile and reviews, then tests real prompts monthly. 11 · FAQ

Roofing SEO: common questions.

What is roofing SEO?

Roofing SEO is the work of getting a roofing company found when homeowners and property managers search for roof repair, roof replacement, metal roofing, commercial roofing or storm damage help in the towns it serves. It covers the Google Business Profile, a page for each job type, service-area pages backed by real local projects, reviews and project photos, technical fixes and tracking leads back to the searches that produced them, across Google Maps, organic results and AI assistants.

What does a roofing SEO company do?

A roofing SEO company researches the searches behind each job type and town, optimizes your Google Business Profile, builds and improves service pages for repair, replacement, metal, commercial and storm work, sets up a review and photo routine, fixes technical issues, adds RoofingContractor schema, tests what AI assistants say about you and reports on calls, inspections and signed contracts rather than rankings alone.

How long does SEO for roofers take to work?

Profile corrections and technical fixes can show an effect within weeks. New service pages, reviews and local authority usually take several months to move rankings, and large metros take longer than smaller towns. Storm pages need to be published and indexed well before your local storm season, because a page written the week after a hailstorm rarely ranks in time. Nobody can guarantee a specific ranking, and we will not.

Should a roofer create a page for every city?

Only for towns where you have real work to show, such as local projects, photos, reviews and details specific to that area. Google's spam policies list pages targeted at specific cities or regions that funnel users to one page as an example of doorway abuse. Towns without that material are better covered by strong job-type pages and the service area on your Google Business Profile.

Should a roofing company show its address on Google?

Most roofers travel to the job, which makes them service-area businesses. Google says that if you don't serve customers at your business address you should remove it and set a service area instead, with up to 20 areas and an overall area of about two hours' driving from your base. A virtual office can't be listed unless it is staffed during business hours.

Are Local Services Ads or SEO better for roofing leads?

They do different jobs. Local Services Ads put you at the top quickly and charge per lead, which helps after a storm or in a new area, and Google lists roofers as an eligible category. SEO builds map pack and organic visibility you do not pay for each time someone calls. Most roofing contractors do best with SEO as the foundation and ads to fill gaps, with both judged on cost per signed job.

How much does roofing SEO cost?

It depends on your market, the job types and towns you want to rank for and how much your website needs. A single-crew roofer in a small town needs far less than a multi-location company competing in a large metro.
