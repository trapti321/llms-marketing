Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what personal information LLM's Marketing ("we", "us") collects through llmsmarketing.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: when you use a form on this website, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, website and message.
  • Booking information: when you book a call, the scheduling tool Calendly collects the details you provide on its booking page.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider and the services we load (such as Google Fonts) may receive standard information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website, for example which pages are viewed and when a form is submitted. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. We do not use advertising cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry or audit request and to prepare for calls you book.
  • To provide services you have asked us for.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Services that process your information

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Bookings are handled by Calendly (calendly.com). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and website usage is measured with Google Analytics (Google LLC). Each provider processes information under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and to manage any working relationship that follows. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email info@llmsmarketing.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date above.
