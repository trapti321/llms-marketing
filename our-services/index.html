02 — How We Work

One process, every service.

Whichever service you start with, the work follows the same four steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We review your website, your competitors and how AI platforms currently describe your brand.

    OutputA visibility audit and a prioritized list of opportunities
  2. 02

    Plan

    We agree on the pages, content, fixes and automations most likely to bring in qualified leads.

    OutputA written roadmap with priorities and owners
  3. 03

    Build

    We fix technical issues, publish content, earn trusted mentions and set up lead follow-up.

    OutputLive improvements on your site and in your funnel
  4. 04

    Measure

    We track rankings, AI mentions, traffic and leads every month, and adjust what is not working.

    OutputA plain-language monthly report
