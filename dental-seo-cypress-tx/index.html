05 — Compliance

Dental marketing that stays compliant.

Dental SEO carries rules most agencies ignore. Under HIPAA, a reply to an online review must not confirm that the reviewer is a patient or mention their treatment, even if they described it themselves. In Texas, 22 TAC §108.57 prohibits dental advertising that is false, misleading, deceptive or not readily verifiable.

So we keep claims verifiable, have a dentist review every clinical statement, avoid unsupported superlatives such as "best" or "painless", and write review replies that thank patients without disclosing anything about their care.

07 — FAQ

Dental SEO in Cypress: common questions.

Do you have an office in Cypress, Texas?

No. LLM's Marketing is based in New York and works with Cypress dental practices remotely, with video calls on your schedule and monthly reporting. Local SEO does not require a local agency office; it requires accurate data about your practice, strong service pages and steady patient reviews.

How long does dental SEO take to show results in Cypress?

Fixes to your Google Business Profile and directory listings can show in local results within weeks. New service pages, reviews and authority usually take three to six months to build, depending on how many practices compete for the same searches in Cypress and the wider Cy-Fair area.

Can you guarantee my practice will rank first in the map pack?

No. Google states that nobody can pay for a better local ranking, and the map pack depends partly on the searcher's distance from your office. Any agency that guarantees first place is overselling. We improve the factors you can influence, relevance and prominence, and report on calls, bookings and visibility every month.

Is dental SEO different from general local SEO?

The foundations are the same, but dental practices carry extra rules. Review replies must not confirm that someone is a patient under HIPAA, and Texas dental advertising must not be false, misleading or unverifiable under the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners rules. We build both into the work.

Do you help with ChatGPT and Google AI Mode as well as Google Maps?

Yes. Patients increasingly ask AI assistants for dentist recommendations. The same complete, consistent practice information that helps your map pack visibility also helps AI systems describe your practice accurately, and we track how AI answers mention you for Cypress dental searches.

What do you need from my practice to get started?

Access to your Google Business Profile, Google Search Console and Google Analytics, a list of the services and insurance plans you want to grow, and 30 minutes with the practice owner or manager. A dentist reviews any clinical content before it goes live.
