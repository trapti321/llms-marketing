01 — The Seattle Market

What makes Seattle SEO different.

Seattle is growing fast. The city passed 800,000 residents for the first time, reaching an estimated 816,600 on April 1, 2025, up 18,900 in a year and the fifth straight year of growth above 2%, according to Washington's Office of Financial Management (KUOW). The wider economy is strong too: the US Bureau of Economic Analysis identified Washington as the fastest-growing state for real GDP in 2024 (BEA). More residents and more businesses mean more searches, and more competitors chasing them.

Geography matters as much as growth. Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence (Google), and in Seattle distance is shaped by water. Lake Washington separates Seattle from Bellevue, Redmond and Kirkland, with only the I-90 and SR 520 bridges between them. The Ship Canal splits north Seattle from downtown, and West Seattle sits across the Duwamish. A business that leads the map pack in Ballard can be invisible to a searcher in Capitol Hill or Bellevue.

That is why we plan Seattle SEO around the areas you can genuinely serve, not one city-wide keyword.

05 — Washington Rules

Marketing within Washington's health data law.

Washington's My Health My Data Act protects consumer health data that falls outside HIPAA. The Attorney General says it generally applies to "all persons and businesses that conduct business in Washington." Most regulated entities had to comply from March 31, 2024, and small businesses from June 30, 2024. A violation is treated as a violation of the state Consumer Protection Act, enforced by the Attorney General and through private lawsuits (Washington Attorney General).

The law also makes it unlawful for anyone to "implement a geofence around an entity that provides in-person health care services" to track visitors, collect their health data or send them health-related ads (RCW 19.373.080).

For dentists, clinics, therapists, med spas, fitness and wellness businesses, this changes how marketing should work. We review which tracking pixels run on health-related pages, avoid location-based ad tactics near care facilities and build visibility through SEO and content, which do not depend on collecting health data. This is not legal advice. Confirm your obligations with a Washington privacy attorney.

09 — FAQ

Seattle SEO: common questions.

How much does SEO cost in Seattle?

It depends on your industry, how many services and neighborhoods you want to rank for and how much your website needs. A single-location business targeting a few neighborhoods needs far less than a company competing across Seattle and the Eastside. We scope every engagement after a free audit, so you see the plan and the price before you commit.

How long does SEO take to work for a Seattle business?

Google Business Profile and technical fixes can show results within weeks. New pages, reviews and local links usually take several months to move rankings, and competitive categories such as law, plumbing and HVAC take longer. Nobody can guarantee a specific ranking, and we will not.

Should I target Seattle and the Eastside with the same page?

Usually not. Bellevue, Redmond and Kirkland are separate cities with their own searches and competitors, and Lake Washington makes the drive between them a real factor for customers and for Google's distance signal. If you genuinely serve both, build a strong Seattle page and separate pages for the Eastside cities you cover, each with local details.

Does Washington's My Health My Data Act affect my marketing?

If you handle consumer health data, it may. The Attorney General says the law generally applies to businesses that conduct business in Washington, and it bans geofencing around in-person health care facilities to track people or send them health-related ads. Health, dental and wellness businesses should review their tracking pixels and ad targeting with a Washington privacy attorney.

Do I need a Seattle address to rank in Seattle?

To rank in the map pack, your Business Profile needs a real location or a service area that covers Seattle, and Google weighs distance from the searcher. Organic results are less tied to distance, so useful Seattle pages can rank without an address. Never use a virtual office or mailbox, because Google's guidelines do not allow it.

Do you optimize for ChatGPT and Google AI Mode in Seattle?

Yes. We test how ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity and Gemini answer questions such as "best dentist in Seattle" or "Seattle plumber open now" in your category, then fix the business facts, reviews and pages those answers rely on.

Why hire a remote SEO agency instead of one based in Seattle?

Local knowledge comes from research and data, not from an office address. What matters is whether an agency understands your market, reports on calls and booked work rather than rankings alone, and follows Google's rules. We are open about being based in New York, and we bring the same founder-led process to every Seattle client.
