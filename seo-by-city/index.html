02 — How Local Rankings Work

Why local search is decided city by city.

Google says local results are based mainly on three factors (Google Business Profile Help):

  • Relevance: "how well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for."
  • Distance: "how far each business is from the customer who's searching."
  • Prominence: "how well-known a business is," including links, reviews and ratings.

Distance means the competition changes every few miles. A plumber who leads the map pack in one neighborhood can be invisible across a river or a freeway. Search demand, cost per click, competitors and even the laws that shape your marketing also change from one city to the next. That is why we plan local SEO around specific markets rather than one national checklist.

AI assistants follow the same logic. When someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI Mode for "the best dentist in Seattle," the answer draws on location-specific signals: your Business Profile, reviews, local mentions and pages that clearly describe where you work. Our AI search visibility service covers that side.

03 — No Doorway Pages

One method, no two pages the same.

Many agencies publish hundreds of city pages that are identical except for the city name. Google's spam policies call this doorway abuse, and list "having multiple domain names or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" and "creating substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy" as examples (Google Search Central).

We only build a city page when we can make it genuinely useful for that market. Every page in this directory, and every city page we build for clients, has to clear the same bar:

  • Search demand and cost-per-click data pulled for that city
  • The neighborhoods, suburbs and geography that shape who can reach you
  • Local and state rules that change how you can market there
  • Services and proof that match what customers in that market buy
  • A Google Business Profile set up within Google's guidelines, never a virtual office

05 — Which Model Fits

One location or many, the rules differ.

Single-location businesses

If you have one office, store or service base, your Business Profile can only rank well near where you are. We strengthen that profile, build pages for the neighborhoods and suburbs you serve from it, and earn local mentions that make you the obvious choice nearby. Google says service-area businesses that visit customers should hide their address and list the areas they serve, and we set profiles up that way.

Multi-location brands and franchises

Each real, staffed location can have its own Business Profile and its own location page. We build those pages with local details, staff, reviews and directions for each site, keep business facts consistent everywhere and report performance location by location. 07 — FAQ

SEO by city: common questions.

What is SEO by city?

SEO by city is local search optimization planned around a specific market. Instead of one generic strategy, it uses that city's search demand, competitors, neighborhoods and local rules to decide which services, areas and keywords to target, so a business shows up in the map pack, organic results and AI answers where its customers are.

Do I need a separate page for every city?

Only for cities you genuinely serve and can say something useful about. A separate page makes sense when you have customers, staff, projects or a location there. Pages that only swap the city name are what Google calls doorway abuse, and they rarely rank or convert.

Can I rank in a city where I don't have an office?

In organic results, yes, if your pages are genuinely useful for people in that city. In the map pack it is harder, because Google weighs distance from the searcher. Google's guidelines do not allow virtual offices or fake addresses, so the honest route is a real location or a service-area profile that lists the areas you visit.

How long does local SEO take in a new city?

Business Profile and technical fixes can show results within weeks. New pages, reviews and local links usually take several months to move rankings, and large, competitive metros take longer than smaller cities. Nobody can guarantee a specific ranking, and we will not.

Do you only work with businesses in the cities listed?

No. The directory shows the markets we have written detailed guides for so far, and we add more regularly. We work with businesses across the United States, so if your city is not listed, request an audit and we will research your market.

Do you optimize for AI search as well as Google?

Yes. Every city program includes testing how ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity and Gemini answer local questions in your category, then fixing the business facts, reviews and pages those answers rely on. Google says there are no special requirements for its AI features beyond strong SEO fundamentals.
