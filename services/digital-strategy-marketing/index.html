AI SEO & Content Services

Get found on Google and recommended in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.

AI SEO & Content is our core search service. We improve your website and content so that your business shows up when people search on Google and when they ask AI assistants for recommendations. It is built for service businesses, SaaS companies and online brands that already have a website but are not getting enough qualified visitors or enquiries from search. The outcome we work towards is simple: more of the right people finding you, trusting what they read, and getting in touch.

What's Included

  • Search and AI visibility audit: we check how your site performs on Google and how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews.
  • Keyword and question research: we find the searches and AI prompts your buyers use before they choose a provider.
  • Content plan and writing: clear, useful pages and articles that answer those questions better than your competitors do.
  • On-page optimization: titles, headings, internal links and page structure, so search engines and AI tools understand each page.
  • Technical SEO fixes: crawling, indexing, site speed and structured data (schema) problems that hold your site back.
  • Monthly reporting: rankings, organic traffic, leads and AI mentions, explained in plain language.

How It Works

  1. Audit: we review your website, your competitors and your current visibility in search and AI answers.
  2. Plan: we agree on a prioritized list of fixes and content, starting with what is most likely to bring in leads.
  3. Build: we fix technical issues, improve existing pages and publish new content.
  4. Measure and improve: we track results every month and adjust the plan based on what is working.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is AI SEO different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO focuses on ranking your pages in Google's results. AI SEO also looks at whether AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity mention or cite your brand when people ask for recommendations. Most of the work overlaps: useful content, a technically healthy website and a credible brand help with both.

How long does it take to see results?

Technical fixes and improvements to existing pages can show an effect within weeks. New content and brand authority usually take several months to build. The timeline depends on your website, your competition and how much content needs to be created.

Can you guarantee rankings or ChatGPT recommendations?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a specific ranking or a mention in an AI answer, because search engines and AI platforms decide what to show. What we can do is improve the signals that make your brand easier to find, understand and trust, and report clearly on progress.
