01 — The Problem

The problems we fix.

More of your buyers now start their research by asking an AI assistant a question instead of scrolling through search results. They ask which agency, tool or provider fits their situation, and the assistant answers with a short list of names. If your business is not on that list, or is described incorrectly, you lose the inquiry before you ever knew it existed. Our AI search visibility service is built to fix that: we find out where you stand today, work out why competitors are being recommended instead of you, and improve the signals AI platforms rely on when they choose which brands to mention.

Our LLM optimization services are led by Mohd Adil Kazmi, an SEO consultant and founder with more than eight years of experience running SEO experiments that test what actually changes visibility in search and AI answers. That means our recommendations are based on what we have seen work, not on guesswork or hype. The work itself is practical: clearer pages, consistent brand information across the web, useful content that answers real buyer questions, and credible mentions on sites AI tools trust. We report progress in plain language every month, so you always know what we did and what changed.

05 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is AI search visibility?

AI search visibility is how often, and how accurately, your brand appears in answers from AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. It covers being mentioned by name, being cited as a source and being described correctly. It matters because many buyers now shortlist providers based on these answers.

How is LLM optimization different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO focuses on ranking your pages in search results. LLM optimization focuses on whether AI assistants mention, recommend or cite your brand when people ask questions. The two overlap a lot, since clear content, a healthy website and a credible brand help with both, but AI visibility also depends heavily on what other trusted sites say about you.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my brand?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a mention in an AI answer, because each platform decides what to show and answers vary between users and over time. What we can do is improve the signals that make your brand easier to find, understand and trust, and report clearly on what changes.

How long does it take to see a change?

Fixing inaccurate brand information and improving key pages can show an effect within weeks on platforms that search the web live. Building enough content and outside mentions to change recommendations across many questions usually takes several months. The timeline depends on your market, your competitors and your starting point.

Which AI platforms do you track?

We focus on the platforms your buyers are most likely to use, typically ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. We agree the list and the set of buyer questions with you at the start, so reports stay consistent month to month.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short call about your services, customers and main competitors, plus access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics. If we will be editing your website, we also need access to your content management system or a contact on your team who can publish changes.
