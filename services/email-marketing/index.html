New leads get timely, relevant emails that build trust and move them towards a call or a purchase, without your team writing each one by hand.

01 — The Problem

The problems we fix.

Most businesses collect more email addresses than they use. Someone downloads a guide, fills in a contact form or signs up for updates, and then hears nothing for weeks, or receives the same generic newsletter as everyone else. Our email marketing automation services turn that list into a steady source of conversations. We plan and write automated email sequences that send the right message at the right moment: a welcome series for new subscribers, a nurture sequence for leads who are not ready yet, and follow-ups after a call, a quote or a missed booking.

Good automated email feels personal because it responds to what each person has actually done. We set up triggers based on sign-up source, pages visited, forms submitted and replies, and keep your list clean so your emails keep reaching inboxes. Copy is clear and useful, written in your brand's voice, and focused on one next step per email. We test subject lines, timing and content, then report on what turns readers into inquiries, so the program improves month by month.

05 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What are email marketing automation services?

They cover planning, writing and setting up emails that send automatically based on what a subscriber or lead does. Instead of sending every email by hand, you have sequences that welcome new contacts, nurture interested leads and follow up after key actions. We handle the strategy, copy, setup and ongoing improvement.

Do you write the emails?

Yes. We write the copy in your brand's voice and share it for your approval before anything goes live. If your team prefers to write some emails, we can edit and structure their drafts instead.

Can you guarantee open rates or sales from email?

No. Results depend on your list, your offer and your audience, and inbox providers decide how emails are filtered. What we can do is follow good list and sender practices, write useful emails and report honestly on replies, bookings and sales.

How soon will the first sequences go live?

That depends on how many sequences you need and how quickly copy is approved. We usually start with the sequence closest to revenue, such as follow-up after an inquiry, so it can start working while we build the rest.

Which email platform do you use?

We work with the email platform you already use where it can support the automations you need. If it cannot, we explain the limitation and recommend options, but we do not require you to switch.

How is this different from sending a newsletter?

A newsletter goes to everyone at the same time. Automated sequences go to each person at the moment that matters for them, based on what they did, which makes them more relevant. Many businesses use both, and we can help with each.
