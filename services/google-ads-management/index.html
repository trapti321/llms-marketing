01 — The Problem

Why most Google Ads accounts waste money.

Google Ads management is the ongoing work of planning, building, measuring and improving paid campaigns on Google Search, Shopping, YouTube and the wider Google network. Google makes it easy to launch a campaign in an afternoon. It is much harder to make one profitable, because the platform will spend whatever budget you give it whether or not the clicks turn into customers.

Clicks are getting more expensive. LocaliQ's 2026 search advertising benchmarks put the average cost per click across industries at $5.42, with attorneys and legal services at $9.87 and home and home improvement at $8.33 (LocaliQ, June 2026). The same report found an average conversion rate of 8.18%, which means roughly nine in ten paid clicks do not become a lead. When that many clicks are lost anyway, the ones lost to bad targeting, broken tracking or a weak landing page decide whether ads make money.

Our approach is simple: measure what a customer costs, then spend only where that number works.

04 — The Audit

Where ad budgets quietly leak.

Most accounts we review lose money in the same four places, and each one is checked against Google's own documentation.

Irrelevant search terms

Keywords are not the same as searches. Google's search terms report shows the actual searches that triggered your ads, and it often includes job hunters, do-it-yourselfers and people looking for something free. Negative keywords stop your ads from showing for those searches.

Broken conversion tracking

Automated bidding learns from the conversions you report. If a page view, a duplicate form submission or a 5-second call counts as a lead, Google optimizes toward the wrong thing. We count real leads, then feed back which ones became customers through offline conversion imports.

Homepage as the landing page

Google's Quality Score is based on expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. Sending every ad to your homepage hurts the last two, and it makes searchers hunt for the answer they clicked for.

Wrong places and hours

Location settings that include people "interested in" your area, or ads running overnight when nobody answers the phone, buy clicks that cannot become customers.

05 — Ads in AI Search

Google Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode.

Google now shows ads inside its AI answers. According to Google Ads Help, existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to serve ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode, which are available in English in the United States and select English-speaking markets (Google).

How you target decides where you can appear. Google says AI-powered targeting, including AI Max for Search, broad match, Shopping and Performance Max, can be matched to both the query and the AI response. Exact and phrase match keywords are eligible only when the query itself shows clear commercial intent, not on broader exploratory questions. You cannot target AI Overviews on their own or opt out of them separately from Search, but negative keywords and brand exclusions still apply.

That makes clean conversion data and strong negative lists more important, not less. 09 — FAQ

Google Ads management: common questions.

How much do Google Ads management services cost?

There are two costs: your ad spend, paid directly to Google, and the management fee, paid to the agency. Agencies charge a flat monthly fee, a percentage of ad spend or a hybrid of both. The right fee depends on how many campaigns, services and locations you run. We scope every engagement after a free audit, so you see the plan and the price before you commit.

How much should I spend on Google Ads each month?

Work backward from your numbers. Multiply the leads you want each month by your expected cost per lead, then check that a typical click cost in your category buys enough clicks to learn from. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the average cost per click at $5.42 across industries, but high-intent searches such as personal injury or emergency plumbing cost far more. A budget too small to get meaningful clicks each week makes it hard to tell what works.

How long does it take for Google Ads to work?

Ads can bring clicks the day they go live, but profitable campaigns take longer. Expect the first few weeks to be spent fixing tracking, cutting irrelevant searches and gathering conversion data, and automated bid strategies need conversion history to perform well. Most accounts are judged fairly after two to three months of clean data.

Can you guarantee results from Google Ads?

No. Nobody controls Google's auction, your competitors' bids or how your team handles the leads. What we can do is track real leads accurately, stop spend on searches that do not convert, improve landing pages and report honestly on cost per lead every month.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

You should. We recommend that the account, billing and advertiser verification stay in your business's name, with us given access to manage it. That way your conversion history and data stay with you if you ever change agencies.

Should I use Performance Max or Search campaigns?

It depends on your goal and data. Search campaigns give the most control over which searches you pay for, which suits most lead-generation businesses. Performance Max runs across Google's channels from one campaign and can work well for ecommerce with a product feed or accounts with strong conversion data. Many accounts use both, with brand exclusions and clear reporting for each.

Can my ads show in Google AI Overviews and AI Mode?

Yes. Google says existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to show ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode in the United States and select English-speaking markets. Broad match, AI Max, Shopping and Performance Max can match exploratory questions, while exact and phrase match keywords show only for clearly commercial queries. You cannot target or opt out of these placements separately.

Should I invest in Google Ads or SEO?

They solve different problems. Google Ads buys visibility immediately and stops when the budget stops. SEO and AI search visibility take longer but lower your cost per customer over time. Most businesses do best using ads for speed and high-value services while organic visibility builds, which is why we plan both together.
