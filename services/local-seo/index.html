01 · How Local Ranking Works

What Google says decides local rankings.

Local SEO is the work of making a business easy to find, trust and choose for searches with a place attached, such as "dentist near me," "emergency electrician Chicago" or "best HVAC company open now." Most of those results are decided in the local pack and Google Maps, not the ten blue links.

Google is unusually direct about how this works. Its Business Profile help center says local results "are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity," and describes the three factors as relevance, distance and prominence (Google Business Profile Help). The same page says there is "no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google."

Relevance

How well your profile matches the search. Google says complete, detailed business information helps it match you to relevant searches. In practice that means the right primary category, accurate services, a useful description and website pages that back them up.

Distance

How far you are from the person searching. You cannot move your building, but you can make sure your address or service area is accurate, that each real location has its own verified profile, and that Google is never confused by duplicates or old addresses.

Prominence

How well known you are. Google says prominence draws on information like how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have, and that "more reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." This is where reviews, local links and local press do their work.

03 · Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile optimization done by the rules.

Your Business Profile is usually the first thing a nearby customer sees, often before your website. Google's own ranking tips are to verify the business, keep information complete and accurate, respond to reviews and add photos and videos (Google). That sounds basic, and it is, yet incomplete hours, the wrong primary category and outdated phone numbers are among the most common problems we find.

We are equally careful about what not to do. Google's guidelines say your business name "should reflect your business's real-world name" and that adding unnecessary information to it can lead to suspension. They also say virtual offices are not eligible for a profile, P.O. boxes are not acceptable addresses, and service-area businesses should have one profile for their central office with a designated service area (Google Business Profile guidelines). Keyword-stuffed names and fake locations can work for a few weeks, then cost you the listing entirely.

What we set up and maintain

  • Primary and secondary categories chosen from competitor and search data
  • Services, products and a plain-English description of what you do and where
  • Regular hours, special hours for holidays and accurate attributes
  • Real photos of your team, work and premises, added on a regular schedule
  • Posts for offers, events and seasonal services where they help customers decide
04 · Google Reviews

More Google reviews, earned honestly.

Reviews affect both ranking and choice. Google lists review count and ratings under prominence, and customers lean on them heavily. BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey of 1,002 US adults found that 41% now always read reviews when choosing a local business (up from 29% in 2025), 74% look for reviews written in the last three months, and 80% are more likely to use a business that replies to all of its reviews (BrightLocal survey, as reported by PinMeTo). Recency and replies matter, not just the star average.

The rules are strict, and we follow them. Google says offering incentives such as discounts in exchange for posting, changing or removing reviews "is strictly prohibited," and recommends asking customers through a review link or QR code and replying to reviews (Google). In the US, the FTC's final rule on reviews bans buying fake reviews, offering incentives conditioned on a particular sentiment and using threats to suppress negative reviews, and lets the agency seek civil penalties against knowing violators (FTC).

So our review work is operational: we build the ask into your existing workflow (the invoice, the follow-up text, the end of the appointment), make it one tap for the customer, and help your team reply in a human voice. No gating, no paid reviews, no review swaps.

05 · Website, Citations and Links

Location pages, citations and local links.

Location and service pages that deserve to rank

Your website supports relevance and gives Google, and AI tools, something to cite. Each real location gets a page with its own address, hours, staff, photos, reviews, directions and the services offered there. Each core service gets a page that answers the questions customers ask before they call. We are careful here because Google's spam policies name "having multiple domain names or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" as doorway abuse (Google Search Central). If you do not have a presence in a city, we will not pretend you do.

Local citations and NAP consistency

Citations are mentions of your business name, address and phone number (NAP) on other sites, such as data aggregators, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp and industry directories. Their biggest value today is accuracy: a wrong phone number on a popular directory sends real customers to the wrong place and muddies the information search engines and AI tools pull from. We audit, correct and remove duplicates rather than mass-submitting to hundreds of low-value sites.

Local links and mentions

Because Google ties prominence partly to how many websites link to you, we pursue links a genuine local business earns: suppliers and manufacturers you are certified with, local associations, sponsorships, community events, local news coverage and partner businesses. 06 · Local AI Answers

Showing up when people ask AI for a local business.

More local searches now start with a question, such as "who is a reliable AC repair company near me that is open on Sundays?" Google's guidance for AI Overviews and AI Mode says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations to appear, that normal SEO best practices still apply, and specifically recommends checking that your Business Profile information is up to date (Google Search Central). In other words, strong local SEO is the foundation for local AI visibility.

ChatGPT is also building local tools for businesses. 12 · FAQ

Local SEO services: common questions.

What do local SEO services include?

Local SEO services cover everything that affects how you appear in Google Maps, the local pack and local AI answers: a local SEO audit, Google Business Profile optimization, a Google reviews program, location and service pages, LocalBusiness schema, citation and NAP cleanup, local link building, call and lead tracking and monthly reporting. Each task should support one of Google's three documented local ranking factors: relevance, distance and prominence.

How long does local SEO take to work?

Fixes such as correcting a wrong category, missing hours or duplicate listings can show an effect within weeks once Google processes them. Building prominence through reviews, links and content takes longer, and most businesses should judge a local SEO program after about three to six months. Competitive categories in large cities take longer than niche services in smaller towns.

Can you guarantee a top 3 spot in the map pack?

No. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking, and distance from the searcher is outside anyone's control, so rankings vary across your service area. What we can do is fix what Google documents as important, track rankings across your area rather than one point, and report honestly on calls and leads.

How do I get more Google reviews without breaking the rules?

Ask every customer, make it easy with a Google review link or QR code, and reply to reviews. Do not offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews: Google prohibits incentives for posting, changing or removing reviews, and the FTC's rule bans incentives conditioned on positive or negative sentiment and buying fake reviews. Our guide on how to get more Google reviews covers the full process.

Do I need a separate page for every city I serve?

Only for locations you genuinely operate from or serve with real, specific content. Google's spam policies treat pages targeted at specific cities that funnel users to one page as doorway abuse. A service-area business is usually better served by one strong page per service, a clear service area on its Business Profile and location pages only where it has a real presence.

How is local SEO different for multi-location businesses?

The ranking factors are the same, but each real location needs its own verified Business Profile, its own location page and its own review flow. The common problems are duplicates, closed locations still showing, inconsistent phone numbers and cloned location pages, so multi-location work leans more on standards, monitoring and location-level reporting.

Does local SEO help me show up in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode?

Yes, it is the foundation. Google says there are no special requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode and recommends keeping your Business Profile up to date. ChatGPT now lets businesses claim a listing through its Business Hub. Accurate profiles, reviews, citations and clear service pages give AI tools correct information to draw from, and we test local prompts monthly to check what they say about you.
