01 — The Problem

The problems we fix.

As a business grows, its marketing tends to grow in layers. A form here, a spreadsheet there, an email tool, a CRM, a calendar link, and a team member who copies details between them by hand. It works until it does not: a lead gets missed, a follow-up never goes out, or nobody can say which campaign produced last month's customers. As a marketing automation agency, we untangle that. We map how leads and customer information move through your business today, find the manual steps and gaps, and replace them with reliable automated workflows.

We focus on automations that save real time or recover lost revenue, not on building complexity for its own sake. That usually means sending new leads to the right person instantly, triggering follow-ups when someone fills in a form or books a call, keeping your CRM accurate without manual entry, and giving you reporting you can trust. We document every workflow in plain language, so your team understands what runs, when and why, and is not dependent on us to make small changes later.

05 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does a marketing automation agency do?

We connect the tools you use to capture, nurture and convert leads, and set up workflows that run without manual effort. That includes routing new leads, triggering follow-ups, keeping your CRM accurate and tracking where customers come from. The aim is fewer missed opportunities and less time spent on admin.

Do we need to switch to new software?

Usually not. We work with the tools you already have wherever possible and only suggest a change when a tool genuinely cannot do what you need. If we do recommend something new, we explain why and what it would cost before you decide.

Can you guarantee a specific return from automation?

No. Automation removes manual work and makes follow-up more consistent, but the commercial result depends on your offer, your leads and your team. We agree what to measure at the start, such as time saved or response speed, and report on it honestly.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on how many tools and workflows are involved. A focused project, such as lead routing and follow-ups, is quicker than rebuilding your whole CRM process. We give you a timeline once the workflow audit is complete.

What happens if an automation breaks?

We build in error alerts and checks so problems are spotted quickly rather than silently. During our engagement we fix issues as they come up, and the documentation we provide helps your team understand and resolve common problems on their own.

Can marketing automation work alongside AI lead agents?

Yes, and they work well together. An AI lead agent handles the first conversation and qualification, and automation takes over from there with CRM updates, routing to the right person and follow-up sequences.
