Every inquiry gets an instant, accurate answer, the right leads get booked onto your calendar, and your team spends its time on sales conversations.

01 — The Problem

The problems we fix.

Most businesses lose leads in the gap between an inquiry and a reply. A visitor asks a question on your website at 9pm, nobody answers until the next morning, and by then they have contacted a competitor. Our AI lead generation agents close that gap. We build an assistant that sits on your website chat and other inquiry channels, answers questions about your services using your own information, asks the qualifying questions your sales team would ask, and books suitable prospects straight into a call. Every conversation is logged in your CRM so nothing slips through.

We design each agent around how your business actually sells. We start by learning your services, pricing rules, ideal customers and the questions prospects ask most, then write the agent's knowledge and conversation flow from that. We set clear limits on what it should and should not say, decide when it hands a conversation to a person, and test it thoroughly before launch. After launch we review real conversations and keep improving answers and qualification rules, so the agent gets more useful over time instead of drifting.

05 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does an AI lead generation agent actually do?

It answers inquiries on your website chat and other channels, asks qualifying questions, books suitable prospects into a call and logs everything in your CRM. Think of it as a first-response assistant that works around the clock and follows the rules you set. Your team still handles the sales conversations that matter.

Will it give customers wrong information?

We limit the agent to information you approve and write clear instructions about what it must not answer, such as custom quotes or legal advice. When a question falls outside those limits, it hands over to a person instead of guessing. We also review conversations after launch to catch and correct weak answers.

Can you guarantee more leads?

No. An agent can respond faster and qualify more consistently, but the number of leads still depends on your traffic, your offer and your market. What we can promise is a clear report on conversations, qualified leads and bookings, so you can judge the value for yourself.

Does it work with our existing CRM and calendar?

In most cases, yes. We connect the agent to the CRM and calendar tools you already use wherever they allow it, and confirm this during discovery before any build work starts. If a direct connection is not possible, we agree a practical alternative, such as email notifications or a shared spreadsheet.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how many services, channels and integrations are involved. A focused website chat agent is quicker to launch than one covering several channels and complex qualification rules. We give you a timeline after the discovery session, once we know the scope.

Will visitors know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. We recommend being upfront that the first response comes from an AI assistant, with an easy way to reach a person. Being honest builds trust and avoids frustration when a question needs a human answer.
