01 — The Problem

Why personal injury SEO is harder than most.

Personal injury is one of the most competitive corners of search. Someone hurt in a crash searches with urgency, compares two or three firms in the local pack, reads reviews and calls the first one that looks credible. Large firms with big advertising budgets compete for the same searches, and lead vendors buy their way in front of everyone.

Personal injury lawyer SEO is the work of earning those positions without paying for every click. We focus on what Google says drives local results, relevance, distance and prominence, and on the facts AI assistants rely on when someone asks for a lawyer recommendation: your Google Business Profile, case-type pages, attorney profiles, reviews and consistent listings across legal directories. The work is led by our founder, Mohd Adil Kazmi, an SEO consultant with more than eight years of hands-on search experience.

06 — Compliance

Law firm marketing that respects the ethics rules.

Attorney advertising carries rules most SEO agencies ignore. ABA Model Rule 7.1 prohibits false or misleading communications about a lawyer's services, and many state bars add their own requirements for results claims, testimonials and specialization language.

Reviews need care too. ABA Formal Opinion 496 warns that responding to an online review must not reveal information about a client's representation. So we never invent results, we add the disclaimers your state requires, we avoid "specialist" or "expert" claims unless you are certified, and every page is approved by the responsible attorney before it goes live.

08 — FAQ

Personal injury SEO: common questions.

How much does SEO for a personal injury law firm cost?

It depends on your market, the case types you want and how much content and technical work your site needs. Ranking for car accident cases in a large metro takes more work than a smaller city. We scope every engagement after a free audit, so you see the plan and the price before you commit.

How long does personal injury SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile, citation and technical fixes can show within weeks. Practice-area pages, reviews and authority usually take several months to move rankings in competitive personal injury markets. We report monthly on consultations and signed cases so you can see progress before rankings peak.

Is personal injury SEO different from general law firm SEO?

Yes. Personal injury is one of the most competitive areas in legal search, intent is urgent, and one case can be worth far more than a typical legal matter. That changes the priorities: case-type pages for each injury and accident type, local visibility in every market you serve, fast intake paths, and tracking that ties searches to signed cases.

Can a smaller firm outrank the big billboard firms?

Often, yes, for specific case types and neighborhoods. Large firms tend to compete on broad terms. A smaller firm with complete case-type pages, a strong Google Business Profile, steady reviews and genuinely local content can win searches the big firms cover thinly. Nobody can promise a specific ranking, and we will not.

Do you follow attorney advertising rules?

Yes. Every page is written to avoid false or misleading statements under ABA Model Rule 7.1 and your state bar's advertising rules, results claims carry the disclaimers your state requires, and review responses never disclose client information, in line with ABA Formal Opinion 496. The responsible attorney approves all content before it goes live.

How do we know the cases came from SEO?

We set up call tracking and form attribution by landing page, connect them to your intake process or case management system where possible, and add an AI assistant option to your intake question. Monthly reports show consultations and signed cases by source, not only traffic.
