02 — Why Industry-Specific

Same foundations, different rules.

The fundamentals do not change by industry: Google ranks local businesses on relevance, distance and prominence, and AI assistants rely on consistent, verifiable facts about a business. What changes is everything around them, from the searches customers use to the claims you are allowed to make.

Law firms work within attorney advertising rules and client confidentiality. Dental practices must keep review replies HIPAA-safe and follow state dental board advertising rules. We build those constraints into the work from day one, so your marketing gets results without creating risk.
