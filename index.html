02 — The Problem We Solve

Good businesses are missing from the answer.

More buyers now ask an AI assistant before they ever visit a website. If your brand is not part of that answer, you are not on the shortlist.

We fix the gaps that keep you out: unclear content, inconsistent brand information, weak third-party proof and slow follow-up.

01

Invisible in AI answers

Buyers ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for a recommendation. Your competitors get named. You do not.

02

Ranking, but not cited

Your pages rank on Google, yet AI tools summarize the topic using other sources.

03

Inconsistent brand information

Your website, profiles and listings describe you differently, so AI is unsure what you do.

04

Content that misses real questions

Blog posts target keywords but never answer what buyers actually ask before choosing.

05

Slow lead follow-up

New enquiries wait hours for a reply, and the hottest leads go to whoever answers first.

06

No way to measure it

You can see clicks and rankings, but not how often AI recommends you or what that brings in.
