01 — LLM Marketing, Defined

What is LLM marketing?

LLM marketing is the practice of making your brand easy for large language models to find, understand and recommend, so ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude name you when buyers ask who to choose.

It combines generative engine optimization (GEO), AI SEO, structured data, consistent brand information and trusted third-party mentions. LLM’s Marketing (LLMs Marketing) runs all of it as one plan and measures it in citations, AI referral traffic and leads.
