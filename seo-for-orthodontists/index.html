04 — Local SEO

Local SEO for orthodontists, done by Google's rules.

Orthodontic practices have extra profile rules because Google treats doctors as individual practitioners. Under Google's guidelines, an individual practitioner who is public-facing and can be contacted at the verified location during stated hours can have their own profile, separate from the practice. A practice with one public-facing orthodontist should use a single profile named in the "Practice: Doctor Name" format. Each office needs its own profile, and only locations that are staffed during their stated hours qualify, which matters for satellite offices open a few days a week.

SEO for orthodontists as doctors

Patients choose a doctor as much as a practice. Every orthodontist gets a bio page with education, residency, memberships and board certification. Board certification is voluntary, so it is a real differentiator: we link each certified doctor's bio to the American Board of Orthodontics locator so patients can verify it themselves.

Reviews within HIPAA

We set up a simple routine to request reviews and reply to every one, without confirming that the reviewer is a patient or mentioning their treatment. The stakes are real: a California dental practice paid $23,000 to settle with HHS after its replies to Yelp reviews disclosed patient information. We never buy or write reviews; the FTC's rule on consumer reviews, effective October 21, 2024, prohibits fake reviews and paying for positive ones. Before-and-after smile photos need the patient's signed authorization under the HIPAA Privacy Rule.

10 — FAQ

SEO for orthodontists: common questions.

What is SEO for orthodontists?

SEO for orthodontists is the work of getting an orthodontic practice found when parents and adults search for braces, clear aligners and orthodontists nearby, on Google Maps, in organic results and in AI assistants. It covers Google Business Profiles for each office and orthodontist, a page for each treatment, cost and financing information, HIPAA-safe reviews, technical fixes and tracking consultations back to the searches that produced them.

What does an orthodontic SEO service include?

Our orthodontic SEO services include treatment and location keyword research, Google Business Profiles for each office and public-facing orthodontist, treatment pages for braces, clear aligners, early and adult treatment, office pages, technical SEO and Dentist schema, a HIPAA-safe review routine, AI search visibility testing, conversion improvements such as online consultation booking, and monthly reporting on consultations by treatment and office.

How long does SEO for orthodontists take to work?

Profile and technical fixes can show results within weeks. New treatment pages, reviews and local authority usually take several months to move rankings, and competitive metros take longer than smaller towns. We report monthly on calls and booked consultations so you can see progress before rankings peak. Nobody can guarantee a specific ranking, and we will not.

Should each orthodontist have their own Google Business Profile?

Under Google's guidelines, a public-facing orthodontist who can be contacted at the verified location during stated hours can have an individual practitioner profile, separate from the practice profile. A practice with a single public-facing orthodontist should use one profile named in the format Practice: Doctor Name. Each staffed office should have its own profile.

Can orthodontists reply to online reviews?

Yes, but replies must not confirm that the reviewer is a patient or mention their treatment. HHS has settled with a dental practice whose Yelp replies disclosed patient information. A short, generic reply that thanks the person and invites them to contact the office is the safe approach.

Why hire an SEO agency for orthodontists instead of a general agency?

An orthodontics SEO company should already know the practitioner profile rules, HIPAA limits on reviews and photos, the difference between parent and adult patient journeys, and how cost and treatment searches work. That saves months of learning on your budget. Whoever you hire, ask how they handle doctor profiles and review replies, and how they report consultations rather than just rankings.

How much does SEO for orthodontists cost?

It depends on your market, the number of offices and doctors, the treatments you want to grow and how much your website needs. A single-office practice in a smaller city needs far less than a multi-office group in a large metro. We scope every engagement after a free audit, so you see the plan and the price before you commit.
